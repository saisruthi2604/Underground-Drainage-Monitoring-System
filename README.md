# Underground Drainage Monitoring System 🌍

## 📌 Project Overview

The Underground Drainage Monitoring System is an IoT-based safety and environmental monitoring project designed to monitor underground drainage conditions using multiple sensors.

Underground drainage systems may experience gas accumulation, excessive moisture, abnormal temperature conditions, and potential fire hazards. These conditions can create serious risks for maintenance workers and surrounding infrastructure.

Our system integrates multiple sensors with an Arduino microcontroller to monitor these conditions and provide early warnings.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor environmental conditions inside underground drainage systems.
- Detect harmful gas concentrations.
- Monitor temperature and humidity levels.
- Detect potential fire hazards.
- Improve worker safety through continuous monitoring.

## 🔧 Hardware Components

**Arduino Microcontroller:** Arduino acts as the main processing unit of the system. It receives data from the connected sensors, processes the readings, and controls the output devices based on predefined conditions.

**DHT11 Sensor:** The DHT11 sensor is used to measure the temperature and relative humidity inside the underground drainage environment. It helps monitor changes in environmental conditions.

**MQ-7 Gas Sensor:** The MQ-7 sensor is primarily designed to detect Carbon Monoxide (CO), a toxic gas that can accumulate in confined spaces. It helps identify potentially hazardous gas conditions and improves worker safety.

Water Sensor: The water sensor is used to detect the presence of water. It helps identify water accumulation or possible leakage conditions in the underground drainage system.

**Flame Sensor:** The flame sensor detects the presence of fire by sensing infrared radiation emitted by flames. It acts as a safety component by identifying potential fire hazards in underground drainage areas.

**LCD Display:** The LCD display is used to show the sensor readings and monitoring status. It provides a simple interface for observing the environmental conditions detected by the system.

**Buzzer and LEDs:** The buzzer and LEDs provide audio and visual warning indications when the system identifies abnormal or potentially dangerous conditions.

## ⚙️ Working Principle

1. The sensors continuously monitor underground environmental conditions.
2. The DHT11 measures temperature and relative humidity.
4. The MQ-7 sensor monitors carbon monoxide concentration.
5. The water sensor detects the presence of water and helps identify possible water accumulation or leakage in underground drainage systems.
6. The flame sensor detects the presence of fire.
7. The Arduino processes the sensor readings.
8. The LCD displays the collected information and warnings.
